# v08 — Detached Teleprompter Glass Overlay

Make a detached teleprompter that helps turn a visible sample document into a spoken explanation. It should feel like a thin optical reading aid beside a sheet of paper, not a centered recorder or an always-on-top wall of unreadable transparent text. Preserve the high-refraction overlay identity and the ability to look at the current desktop document while holding to dictate.

## Two objects, unequal jobs

Compose a `100vw × 100vh` page at 1280×800 within the OUTER chooser's macOS traffic-light bezel. There are exactly two initial groups. The background work object is an opaque sample document at x=660, y=104, width=524, height=592, with 40px insets and no interactive website header. Its title is “Sample field guide”; three short paragraphs discuss a fictional coastal walk. This is synthetic context, not a screenshot of a personal desktop.

The detached teleprompter occupies x=144, y=144, width=432, height=504, radius=26. Its 28px optical perimeter is translucent enough to perceive ambient desktop color, but its main text well is opaque and fully legible. A 40px native top action toolbar contains Pin, “Current passage” disclosure and Expand. The only P0 capture button is a 48px rounded control on a leading vertical glass rail centered at x=132, y=300; it shares the teleprompter's optical material, not a second filter. Seven bars sit below it on that rail; Listening/timer text stays in the opaque well. The reading cue line is 80px below the text well's top, with a subtle solid leading marker, never a bright scan line.

P1 prior passages fold into the Current passage disclosure, replacing the well's content rather than stacking a second list below it. P2 refinement route and settings are in Pin's adjacent More popover. Only one passage can be selected. `Command+Option+P` pins/unpins the overlay in the **mock desktop**; the visible consequence is that the overlay stays anchored when the sample document scrolls. Never claim HTML has changed actual window always-on-top state.

Workspace expansion via Expand or `Command+Return` splits the outer canvas into a solid left reading area x=80, y=88, width=500, height=624, and a solid right refined-document area x=628, y=88, width=572, height=624. The prompter frame becomes the left area's native action toolbar and narrow leading capture rail; no duplicate shell. A “Source passages” disclosure temporarily replaces the left reading view with a single-selection passage list. Back/`Command+[` restores the detached overlay and its cue offset, pin state and selected passage. This is a lateral reading/editing expansion, not a dock or source tray.

## Read, dictate, rewrite

Native hints inside the prompter action area read “Hold Option+Space · Text”, “Option+Shift+Space · Todo”, “Escape · Cancel”. They describe sandbox-focused interaction only, not a real global hotkey. Bind physical Space with modifiers, capture intent at first keydown, ignore repeat and release on Space keyup regardless of Option-release order. Holding the rail capture button takes pointer capture and begins immediately. Native Enter/Space on the focused button gives a start/stop toggle alternative; suppress the generated click after pointer hold.

The three exact sample passages are: 1. “The coastal path crosses three sheltered coves. Mark a rest stop before the final headland.” 2. “The sample map uses blue markers for viewpoints. Keep the route description short and clear.” 3. “The fictional visitor center closes at dusk. Finish the sample walk before the final light fades.” Idle selects passage 1. Listening preserves the readable source and opens the well's lower dictation region, **replacing** its unused lower whitespace, not adding another glass card. Stream synthetic speech: “Explain the three coves first, then mention a rest stop before the last headland.” Seven 3px round bars with 3px gaps run through heights 5/10/17/25/17/10/5px at 140ms phase offsets; show Listening and tabular 00:01. Do not auto-scroll the source while the user is holding the capture control; wheel/trackpad and PageUp/PageDown on the source can move the cue manually without cancelling recording.

Release → Refining keeps source intact, sets seven bars to 5px, labels “Preparing a clear explanation” for 1100ms, and prevents duplicate Apply. Done for passage 1 displays the refined rewrite “The sample coastal route visits three sheltered coves. Plan a rest stop before the final headland.” If passage 2 is selected, use synthetic dictation “Keep the blue viewpoints and shorten the route notes” and refinement “Use blue markers for sample viewpoints and keep route notes concise.” For passage 3 use dictation “Say the visitor center closes at dusk and finish the walk before dark” and refinement “The fictional visitor center closes at dusk; finish the sample walk before dark.” Snapshot the passage ID at capture start so the output always belongs to that source. Source reading is then one disclosure away rather than duplicated under the same result. P0 becomes “Apply to sample passage.” Activation replaces only that selected paragraph in the local mock document, with a receipt “Updated passage 1 of 3” using the actual selected passage number. `Command+Z` restores the previous paragraph and its source relationship. Never inject text into an external document. Copy in More is explicit user-only, with full-text selection and “Text selected; press Command+C” fallback.

Todo intent yields one “Prepare the sample coastal guide” task with three folded steps: “Explain the coves”, “Mark a rest stop”, “Review final headland guidance”. P0 becomes “Add to Todo.” It creates exactly one session-memory task only on activation; steps have reversible semantic circular checkring buttons, no checkbox inputs. No task auto-runs. New capture retains committed results.

`Command+L` focuses the sample document; PageUp/PageDown moves its scroll/cue in full-passage increments; `Command+Option+P` toggles mock pin; `Command+Return` expands workspace; `Command+[` returns. The passage disclosure uses Up/Down plus Return to select and reload the source/cue, at rest only. More offers a seeded local refinement failure: raw dictation remains and Retry can re-enter Refining. Escape at rest closes More, then passage lens, then expanded workspace, retaining any applied rewrite.

Escape while Listening or Refining invalidates the run token, clears delayed word/refinement callbacks, restores prior committed result or Idle, announces “Capture cancelled” and returns focus to capture. Pointercancel, lost capture, page hiding and focus loss cancel similarly; ordinary release outside the capture control completes through pointer capture. A late release/callback after cancel is inert. Pin/navigation actions cannot mutate an active recording. A source scroll is not a navigation action and must not accidentally start/stop capture.
