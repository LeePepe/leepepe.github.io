## Voice and runner travel with the selected card
Idle keeps a single seven-bar baseline in the compact tray. Hold>=180ms enters Listening, release starts Refining900ms, Done shows the three-action preview on a SOLID insert sliding from the pocket lip. Short tap/Enter toggles Listening/stop; focused Space held is hold/release. Suppress post-hold click and repeated keydown. Simulated sentence: “整理风栖画册：列出章节，核对页码，写一版目录”. Exactly3 child action items: “列出画册章节”, “核对每章页码”, “写一版目录”. In quick-add context, “收入今日” adds one new parent and those three children once, then selects Today without displacing P0. In selected-parent “语音拆解” context, “应用三步” applies to that parent, with confirmation before replacing any existing children. Preview itself never starts the runner or completes tasks.

Cancel, Escape, pointercancel, lost capture and navigation away return Idle, undo uncommitted changes, invalidate refinement timers and re-stack the tray. Done may be discarded or rerecorded. No microphone, network or personal data. Synthetic AI runner lives inside detail: 待命 → 排队250ms → 第1/3步 → 第2/3步 → 第3/3步 → 完成, 700ms per step. Stop pauses with remaining progress; Continue resumes, Retry resets runner only. Runner status is a short plain line, not a pulsing badge on every card. Runner never silently toggles task completion.
