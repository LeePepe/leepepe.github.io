色轮弧线渐变铁律（Instagram 暖阳弧线，严禁穿心灰黑死区）(Hue-Arc Luminous Gradient Rule)

平台：desktop-macos, mobile-ios · 坐标：color-palette, visual-style, glass-material

原生原语：SwiftUI

渐变色绝不在取色板上走直线或直接混入纯黑 #000000（RGB 直线插值经过色轮中心会产生肮脏的灰黑死区 Muddy Dead Zone）。必须像 Instagram 图标一样沿色轮外圈走同明度/高饱和度『色相弧线』（如暖黄 #FEDA77 -> 珊瑚橙 #F58529 -> 品红 #DD2A7B -> 柔紫 #8134AF），或加入高明度奶白/柔光过渡点。

完整配方、实现与防呆护栏

Never interpolate gradients in a straight RGB line across the color wheel or fade into #000000 (which creates a muddy gray/black dead zone). Step through adjacent hues along a high-luminance arc (Instagram-style warm yellow -> coral -> rose -> violet, or sky blue -> aqua -> mint) with luminous white/pastel stops.

desktop-macos

Use multi-stop `LinearGradient` or `MeshGradient` stepping through adjacent HSB/OKLCH hues at high brightness (`B >= 85%`), never `colors: [accent, .black]`.

SwiftUI 3-to-4 stop `LinearGradient` / `MeshGradient` along a warm or cool hue arc (e.g., `[Color(hex: 0xFEDA77), Color(hex: 0xF58529), Color(hex: 0xDD2A7B)]`), keeping luminance clean behind `.glassEffect`.

Ban 2-stop complementary RGB gradients and `to .black` fades; always insert an intermediate adjacent hue stop to curve around the gray dead zone.

mobile-ios

Use multi-stop `LinearGradient` or `MeshGradient` stepping through adjacent HSB/OKLCH hues at high brightness (`B >= 85%`), never `colors: [accent, .black]`.

SwiftUI 3-to-4 stop `LinearGradient` / `MeshGradient` along a warm or cool hue arc (e.g., `[Color(hex: 0xFEDA77), Color(hex: 0xF58529), Color(hex: 0xDD2A7B)]`), keeping luminance clean behind `.glassEffect`.

Ban 2-stop complementary RGB gradients and `to .black` fades; always insert an intermediate adjacent hue stop to curve around the gray dead zone.
